Sentinel Desk — SIEM & Email Threat Analysis

Built a local Python-based SIEM to collect Windows and firewall events, index and search logs, detect suspicious activity, and manage security alerts. The Email Threat Analysis module examines uploaded email headers and content for indicators of spoofing, credential phishing and sextortion, with explainable risk scores and investigation records.

The email module was inspired by investigating a suspicious message for a client while working part-time, and later analysing a sextortion scam I received myself as a way to practise the same skills. My goal is to make technical security evidence easier to understand.
